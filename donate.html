What your money keeps alive

mdown does one thing: it saves a file from a link you already have access to. There is no account, no library, no ads, and no analytics. Files you open in the local tools — remux, subtitles, metadata — never touch the server at all.

Running it costs about $22 a year: a $12.99 VPS and an $8.55 domain, plus nothing. That number is the whole business model. There is no investor, no growth plan, and no feature held back for a paid tier, because there is no paid tier.

So a donation is exactly what it sounds like — a thank you, not a transaction. Nothing unlocks, nothing gets faster, and nobody gets your email. If you don't donate, the site keeps working and you owe me nothing.
