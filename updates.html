1.3 16 nov 2026 latest

Still on arrival

  • The page no longer fades up from black, the logo no longer drops, and the block no longer rises nine pixels as it arrives. Nothing above the field moves while the page loads.
  • The block sits just below the middle of the window at every height, and stays there: the offset is in svh, so a phone's collapsing toolbar cannot drag it.
  • Pasting a link no longer collapses the hint and lifts the tools underneath. The hint keeps its place, and it is a button: press it and the address of this site goes to your clipboard.
  • The settings sections wrap on a phone instead of scrolling sideways, so server is always on screen.
  • The server no longer writes a link or a tunnel address to its log. The privacy page says exactly what is kept, which is nothing about the download.
  • On a phone the four places at the bottom behave as tabs: settings stops above them, the tip jar opens inside the app, and the lit one follows you.
  • The tip page draws its coins, every crypto row carries its own mark, and every page footer ends with github. Status lives in the top bar, once.
  • The footer keeps its links together in one line, ending on the repository.
  • The way out of a tool is an arrow button the size of the controls beside it, and the logo does the same thing.
  • The uptime card counts in thousands, so a busy server reads 3.3k checks instead of 3,283.
  • The reddit mark faces you: both eyes, a smile, and the antenna straight up.
  • There is a board at the bottom for asking after a tool. It stays a single closed line until you open it, it takes no name, and nothing you write is shown until it has been looked at.
  • The uptime card draws its week once instead of swapping one graph for another under your finger, and it keeps its height while the server answers.
  • The tip jar waits for the settings sheet or the help dialog it came from to leave before it rises, and it leaves on the full slide down instead of snapping away halfway.
  • The site is served through Cloudflare now and refuses to be framed by another origin, while its own phone tabs still frame their page. The www address points at the current GitHub account.
  • When the API does not answer, the page says so once, retries on its own, and clears the line when it comes back.
  • The uptime card reads the server's log in every browser, not the number of times you happened to open it.
  • Downloads and the uptime card come to the front when you touch them, and both stay open.
1.2 15 nov 2026 latest

A finished file waits for you

  • A download no longer lands in your folder on its own. When it finishes, the row grows a save button and stops there. One press, when you are ready. Settings → files → saving still offers save-straight-away, share and copy link.
  • The uptime graph on the status page is the server's own log: one bar a day for thirty days, filled from checks it ran, never from a page visit. The line underneath names the count for today.
  • A phone on its side gets the phone layout now, not a squeezed desktop one.
  • The tip jar is drawn instead of sketched, and the donate page reads top to bottom without a script.
  • The recent strip has a clear button.
  • The phone bar is four plain places to go (downloads, remux, settings, donate) instead of a sliding lens. Taps only, no drag, and it sits on the bottom edge where it belongs.
1.1 14 nov 2026

Phones

  • auto picks from the link: audio for a music service, video for everything else. audio and video force it, and no sound is a switch instead of a fourth button.
  • Paste a link and see the size, running time, resolution and a thumbnail before anything starts. The queue keeps the picture.
  • The sources list lives in the top bar: a dropdown on a desktop, a sheet on a phone, every supported service, no inner scroll.
  • A bottom bar on phones (downloads, remux, settings, donate), and a small progress bar above it, so the queue never covers the page.
  • A file over 250 MB asks before it starts.
  • A bad link gets a red outline and one short shake. Notices cap at three, and hammering fetch can't bury the page.
  • Settings open with the two choices you actually make; the rest sits behind a +. Server is one field, one card, one Check.
  • The three browser tools are under the field, the file preview is the file itself, and the footer carries four links and nothing else.
1.0 12 nov 2026

First release

  • One page: paste a link, choose a mode, save it. No account, no ads.
  • Its own API, with limits on duration, resolution and size.
  • Remux, subtitle extraction and metadata cleaning run in the browser. Those files never leave the device.
  • Pickers for playlists, subtitle tracks, silent video and untouched source audio.

You can run your own copy of the API and keep the downloads on your own machine. Back to the downloader.
